QUESTIONS

Before you apply.

Do I need to have my book idea completely figured out?

No. You need a meaningful area of expertise, experience, or perspective. We use the Discover phase to clarify the message and shape the concept.

Do I have to be a strong writer?

No. You need to be willing to write and engage in the process. The coaching is designed to help you organize and communicate your ideas clearly.

Will Nathan write the book for me?

No. This is a coaching experience, not a ghostwriting service. Your book should sound like you and carry your perspective.

Does the program include publishing?

The program includes publishing strategy and guidance. It does not include the cost or execution of editing, cover design, formatting, ISBNs, or publishing services.

What if I don't finish the manuscript in 12 weeks?

The goal is a completed manuscript, but writing speed varies. We will create a realistic writing plan around your schedule and identify what remains if additional time is needed.

Why is the investment $5,000?

This is a high-touch, private coaching experience designed around your message and your book—not a group course or collection of videos. The value is in the strategy, structure, accountability, and personalized guidance.
